# Marks and anatomy

Use thin marks with clear baselines and recessive axes/grid lines. Anchor
rounded data ends to the baseline (4px radius), use 2px lines, and make point
markers at least 8px when they are interactive. Leave a 2px surface gap between
stacked segments and adjacent bars, and a 2px surface ring where marks overlap.

Use selective direct labels for the values that carry the story rather than
labeling every point. Keep values, legends, and axes in text tokens; the mark
beside them carries series identity. A single-series chart needs no legend
box, while a chart with two or more series always has one.
