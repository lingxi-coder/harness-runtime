Look for the distinction between absolute time and elapsed time, and for the observation
that a lease holder and a lease granter measure different intervals. A strong answer
covers: why a monotonic clock helps and where it still does not; the role of a grace
period and who must pay for it; fencing tokens as an alternative that does not depend on
clocks at all; and what a pause (garbage collection, VM migration) does that skew alone
does not. Penalise an answer that treats NTP as a solution rather than a mitigation.
