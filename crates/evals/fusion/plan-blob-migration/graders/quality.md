Look for verification and reversibility being designed in rather than appended. A strong
plan covers: how the copy is made restartable after a partial failure; what is compared
to call an object verified, and whether that comparison is done from the source or from a
stored digest; how readers are routed during the copy; the point at which the destination
becomes authoritative and what makes it safe; and how long the source is retained after
that. Penalise a plan whose verification step is "spot check" without a stated basis.
