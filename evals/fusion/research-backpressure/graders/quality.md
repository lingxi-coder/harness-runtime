The two constraints together rule out several obvious answers, and a strong response says
so. Look for: blocking the producer versus signalling it; where the bound actually lives
if the producer is a network peer that cannot be blocked; credit-based schemes; spilling
to disk as a way of moving the bound rather than removing it; and what happens when the
producer is itself a consumer of something else. An answer that proposes dropping or an
unbounded buffer without noting it violates a stated constraint should score low.
