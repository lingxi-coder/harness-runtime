Score the candidate higher only when it is genuinely better on the task, not merely
longer. Reward: a consideration the baseline missed entirely, a stated trade-off the
baseline asserted without qualification, or a correction of something the baseline got
wrong. Penalise: repetition, hedging that removes a commitment the baseline made, and
any claim that contradicts the baseline without saying why the baseline was wrong.
Equal quality scores 0.5.
